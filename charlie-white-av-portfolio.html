03 / About

Charlie White

I'm a freelance live sound engineer and AV technician working across live music, school and community events, and corporate functions. I run front-of-house and monitor mixes, design and operate lighting rigs, and build the AV for a room from the ground up — PA, screens, playback and signal routing.

I also take on freelance IT/AV support work, helping venues and event teams get their gear networked, configured and running reliably on the day. Whether it's a one-mic acoustic set or a full stage production, I handle the technical side so the event runs on time and sounds and looks right.
